CLEANPO · ORDER ENTRY FOR WHOLESALE DISTRIBUTORS

Your customers order however they like.
Your team stops retyping it.

Orders come in as emails, PDFs, spreadsheets, texts and photos of handwritten lists. CleanPO reads each one, matches every line to your item numbers and prices, and gives you one clean file your order system imports. Anything uncertain goes to a person, not into your system.

What it checks before anything reaches your system

These checks are plain rules in code, not AI guesses.

Price mismatchLine price differs from your list or contract price
Duplicate orderSame PO sent twice
Unusual quantity500 bags when they normally order 10
Unknown itemNothing in your catalog matches
Discontinued itemProduct no longer active
Unknown customer / credit holdSender not on file, or account on hold
